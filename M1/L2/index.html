Lets learn about different E Bikes

Ridstar Q20

The Ridstar Q20 1500W is a powerful fat-tire electric bike designed for both city riding and light off-road adventures. Its aggressive motorcycle-inspired design makes it stand out compared to regular commuter e-bikes. The bike comes with a 1500W brushless motor and a 48V 20Ah battery, giving riders strong acceleration and the ability to tackle hills, sand, snow, and rough terrain with ease. According to the manufacturer, the bike can reach speeds of around 30–33 mph and travel up to 75 miles with pedal assist.

One of the most popular features of the Q20 is its fat 20-inch tires and full suspension system. These provide comfort and stability when riding on bumpy roads or trails. The bike also includes hydraulic brakes, Shimano 7-speed gears, and an LCD display that shows speed, battery level, and riding information. Riders often enjoy the balance between comfort and power, especially for casual weekend rides and commuting.

However, there are mixed opinions online regarding the exact motor specifications and legality in some countries. Some Reddit users mention that different listings show inconsistent wattage and battery information, which can be confusing for buyers. Others warn that higher-powered versions may not meet e-bike laws in certain regions.

Stark varg

The Stark VARG 80HP is considered one of the most advanced electric motocross bikes ever made. Unlike many e-bikes designed for casual riding, the VARG is a true high-performance motocross machine built to compete with traditional petrol dirt bikes. Its 80-horsepower electric motor delivers instant torque and incredible acceleration, making it faster than many 450cc motocross bikes. The bike is nearly silent compared to petrol bikes, which gives it a unique riding experience both on tracks and trails.

A major reason riders love the Stark VARG is its customization. Through a smartphone-style display mounted on the handlebars, riders can adjust power output, throttle response, engine braking, and riding modes. This allows beginners to reduce power for easier handling while experienced riders can unlock the full 80HP performance. Many riders on Reddit mention that even 60HP is already extremely powerful, and the 80HP version feels almost excessive unless used for aggressive racing.

The VARG also features premium suspension, lightweight construction, and a large battery system designed for motocross performance. Compared to smaller electric dirt bikes, the VARG feels more like a professional race bike. However, its high price means it is usually purchased by serious riders or enthusiasts looking for top-tier electric motocross technology.

Strike Shadow

The Strike Shadow is a budget-friendly electric dirt bike that has become popular among younger riders and beginners. It is often chosen as a cheaper alternative to more expensive bikes like Surron models. The bike is available in different voltage versions, such as 48V, 60V, and 72V, giving riders several power options depending on their experience and budget.

Many people are attracted to the Strike Shadow because of its aggressive styling and decent performance for the price. Riders online describe it as fun, lightweight, and suitable for riding around neighborhoods, trails, or small dirt tracks. Some users on Reddit say the bike performs surprisingly well for its cost and that the battery life is decent for casual riding.

At the same time, the Strike Shadow is not considered as refined or reliable as premium electric dirt bikes. Some riders report issues with brake levers, customer support, or build quality. Because it is aimed at budget-conscious buyers, the materials and components may not be as durable under heavy use. Still, for younger riders or people entering the electric dirt bike world for the first time, the Strike Shadow offers an affordable and exciting option.

Ultra Bee

The Ultra Bee is a larger and more powerful electric off-road motorcycle created by Sur-Ron. It sits between the smaller Light Bee X and full-size motocross bikes like the Stark VARG. Compared to the Light Bee X, the Ultra Bee offers more power, a bigger frame, improved suspension, and higher-speed performance. This makes it suitable for more serious off-road riders who want stronger acceleration and stability at higher speeds.

Many riders describe the Ultra Bee as a perfect middle ground. It is more manageable and lighter than a full motocross bike, but far more capable than smaller electric trail bikes. Riders appreciate its smooth power delivery, strong torque, and versatility on trails, jumps, and open terrain. Some highly modified Ultra Bees can produce enormous power outputs that compete with much more expensive electric motorcycles.

Online discussions often compare the Ultra Bee with the Stark VARG. Riders note that the VARG has more raw power and race-focused engineering, while the Ultra Bee may feel more practical and playful for everyday trail riding. Cooling systems and battery performance are also common discussion points, especially among riders pushing their bikes to extreme speeds or long-distance rides.
